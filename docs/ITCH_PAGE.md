**Title:** Red Painter: OS Edition

**Genre:** Puzzle · **Platform:** Windows · **Tags:** frutiger-aero, y2k, puzzle, casual, painting, relaxing, arcade

## Description
Boot up Red Painter OS: aqua skies, rolling green hills, soap bubbles and glass windows everywhere.

Every board is a grid of tiles, and each tile wants a colour: **FULL** crimson, **HALF** rose or **OFF** water. Paint fast, chain matches into combos and clear boards for time bonuses. Pull off a PERFECT and nothing slips.

But the OS is crawling with trouble:
- **Bleach blobs** smear water over your work. Pop them.
- **Spiller blobs** drip red where you don't want it.
- **Shifting tiles** change their minds every few seconds.

Catch the floating **power bubbles**: Freeze time, Bucket fill, Mega brush, Rinse and +6 seconds.

Want to relax instead? **Zen mode** removes the clock, the lives and the blobs, and swaps in underwater music.

Under the glass:
- The canvas is scanned on every CPU core ten times a second. Open **Benchmark Lab** to watch the speed-up.
- Adaptive FMOD Studio audio: the music opens up as the canvas gets redder, and gets tense when the clock runs low.
- Press **F3** for live audio telemetry.

**Controls:** mouse to paint and pop · right-click to wash · 1/2/3 brushes · wheel to resize · Esc to pause
